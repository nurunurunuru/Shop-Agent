Privacy Policy

This policy explains how our shop ("we") handles information when you message us on Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp or comment on our posts.

Information we collect

Your name or username, the messages, images and voice notes you send us, and the delivery details (name, phone, address) you give us to place an order.

How we use it

To answer your questions, process and deliver your orders, share parcel tracking updates, and improve our service. Replies are generated with the help of an AI service (Google Gemini) and may be reviewed by our team.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share delivery details with courier partners only to deliver your order, and use Meta and Google services to run the chat.

Retention and deletion

We keep chat and order records as long as needed for support and accounting. To access or delete your data, message us on any of our channels and we will remove it.

Contact

Message our Facebook Page or the channel you used to reach us.
