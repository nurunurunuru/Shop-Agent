Privacy Policy

This service lets online shops ("Shops") answer their customers on Facebook Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp, take orders and manage them from one dashboard. This policy covers two groups of people: Shop owners and team members who use the dashboard, and the customers who message a Shop.

Shop owners and team members

When you create an account we store your name, email and a hashed password. When you connect a Facebook Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number through Meta's login, we receive the Page, Instagram or WhatsApp identifiers and an access token that lets us read and send messages for those accounts only. We use them solely to run the service for your Shop. Tokens are stored on our server and are never shown in the browser or included in backups.

Customers who message a Shop

We process, on behalf of the Shop, your name or username, the messages, images and voice notes you send, and the delivery details (name, phone, address) you give to place an order. Replies are drafted with the help of an AI service (Google Gemini) and may be reviewed by the Shop's team. The Shop is responsible for its customer data; we act as its service provider.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Delivery details are shared with the Shop's chosen courier only to deliver an order. We use Meta and Google services to run the chat and AI features.

Retention and deletion

Shops: you can disconnect any Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number from the dashboard at any time, which stops message processing and removes its token. Removing the app from your Facebook settings, or using Meta's data deletion request, also deletes the stored access tokens for your connected accounts automatically. To delete your whole account and its data, contact us using the details below.

Customers: to access or delete your data, message the Shop on the channel you used and it will remove it.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the service operator at the email listed on the website where you signed up.
